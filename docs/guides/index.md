ChatGPT in a web browser cannot use sourcebolt. sourcebolt runs on your own computer, so it needs an app installed on your computer.

## Before you start

You need:

- A Google account that can open [NotebookLM](https://notebook.google.com).
- Brave, Chrome or Edge installed. sourcebolt opens one of them so you can sign in to Google.
- About 2 minutes.

Your Google password stays with Google. You type it only into Google's own sign-in page, never into a terminal or a chat.
